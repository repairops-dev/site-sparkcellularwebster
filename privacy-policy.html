Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spark Cellular LLC ("Spark Cellular", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or bring a device to our shop in Webster, Texas.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number and email address.
  • Device details, such as make, model, condition and the issue you describe.
  • Payment information, which is handled by our payment processors. We do not keep full card numbers.
  • Basic website data, such as browser type, pages visited, approximate location and cookie data.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer your request and contact you about it.
  • To evaluate and service your device and keep service records.
  • To process payments and handle warranty claims.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our business secure.

3. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed, with:

  • Payment processors that handle your transactions.
  • Companies that help us run the business, such as the provider that receives website form requests and delivers them to our team.
  • Advertising and analytics providers, which may receive website usage data to measure ad performance.
  • Law enforcement or authorities when the law requires it.

4. Cookies and advertising

This website may use cookies and similar tools, including tools from advertising platforms such as Google, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data on your device

We do not intentionally access your photos, files or accounts during service. Some services may require testing device functions. Please back up your data before service. Spark Cellular is not responsible for data loss.

6. How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires.

8. Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to stop sending you marketing messages at any time.

To make a request, call us or visit the shop. We may need to confirm your identity first.

9. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Brand affiliation

Spark Cellular is an independent business and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorized by Apple, Samsung, Google, Motorola, LG or any other manufacturer. See our Brand Disclaimer.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
