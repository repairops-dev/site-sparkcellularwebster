Warranty, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We want you to know exactly what is covered before you pay. This page explains our limited warranty, when refunds apply and how cancellations work.

1. Upfront pricing and approval

You receive an estimate before any service begins. No work is done without your approval, and you can decline service at that point at no cost.

2. Limited warranty

Most hardware services include a 90 day limited warranty on parts and labor, starting on the date the service is completed. The warranty covers defects in the parts we installed and workmanship on the service we completed.

3. What the warranty does not cover

  • New physical or accidental damage after service, including cracks, pressure damage or bent frames.
  • Liquid or moisture exposure.
  • Software issues, account locks or data loss.
  • Issues unrelated to the original service.
  • Damage from work done by another provider after our service.

4. How warranty claims work

Bring the device and your receipt to the shop. We check the device, and if the issue is covered we will service it again or replace the covered part at no charge.

5. Refunds

If we cannot complete an approved service, you are not charged for that service. Once a service is completed and the device works as discussed, covered issues are handled under the warranty rather than by cash refund, unless the law requires otherwise.

6. Cancellations

You can cancel before service begins at no charge. If parts were ordered specially for your device, we will tell you about any non refundable part cost before ordering.

7. Important notes

  • Please back up your data before service. Spark Cellular is not responsible for data loss.
  • Manufacturer warranties are separate from our warranty, and outside service may affect them.
  • Spark Cellular is an independent business and is not affiliated with any device manufacturer. See our Brand Disclaimer.
