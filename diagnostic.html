Warranty and policies

Know what is covered before you pay.

90 day limited warranty

Most hardware services include a 90 day limited warranty on parts and labor for the work we completed.

Covered

  • Defects in the parts we installed
  • Workmanship on the completed service
  • Display or touch faults related to an installed part

Not covered

  • New physical damage after service
  • Liquid exposure
  • Software issues or data loss
  • Issues unrelated to the original service
  • Damage from later work by another provider
